# Hi there, I'm Dr. Yu Min Syu 👋

**Pediatric Geneticist & Endocrinologist | Data Science & AI in Healthcare**

I am a physician-scientist dedicated to bridging the gap between clinical medicine and data science. With clinical expertise in pediatric genetics and endocrinology, and a Master's degree in Data Science, I focus on leveraging machine learning and LLMs to solve complex healthcare challenges and optimize clinical workflows.

📄 **[Download My Full CV (PDF)](./Yu_Min_Syu_CV_2026.pdf)**

### 🔭 Current Focus & Research
* **Clinical NLP & LLM:** Developing an automated phenotype extraction system from electronic medical records (EMR) to support genetic analysis and clinical decision-making.
* **Genomics & GWAS:** Researching the genetic architecture of body height using machine learning approaches in underrepresented populations (Taiwan Biobank).
* **Pediatric Growth and Puberty:** Exploring mechanisms of human height and pubertal onsets; modeling children's growth via environmental factors and genetic variations.
